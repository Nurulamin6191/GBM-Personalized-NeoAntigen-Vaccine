# Limitations (explicit) and Future Directions

Every claim in this repo is traceable to an evidence file. Equally, every boundary
below is load-bearing for interpretation — read before citing Table 1.

## 1. Tumor-only design, no matched normal
- **Limitation:** without patient-matched normal DNA, germline variants leak into
  calls. Mitigated (not solved) by the dbSNP rsID proxy: 49.9% of 4,265 missense
  removed as common germline (04_Variants/missense_dbsnp.tsv). Rare germline
  variants remain indistinguishable from somatic.
- **Future:** tumor–normal whole-exome sequencing + Mutect2 somatic calling with
  panel-of-normals; re-gate Table 1 on somatic-only status.

## 2. Pilot scope: chr1-curated, genome-wide partially re-derived
- **Limitation:** curated variant evidence is chr1/FreeBayes-centric; the FreeBayes
  VCF is genome-wide but its source alignment is unarchived (VALIDATION provenance
  flag). Local GATK chain is chr1-only. Full-genome HISAT2 BAMs + StringTie TPM
  exist as method, but a full-genome variant call was not completed here.
- **Future:** GATK RNA-seq or Mutect2-tumor-only on results-RUNTIME/align_full BAMs;
  joint QC with the archived VCF.

## 3. SDF4 A120V excluded (triple-strike, documented — not a candidate)
- FreeBayes-only (2 ALT reads, VAF 6%, QUAL~0), GATK-absent ±1 kb, carries
  rs1384393162 (germline), and its 9mers do not bind HLA-A*02:06 (all >6,800 nM).
  Any vaccine claim on SDF4 from this data would be unsupported.

## 4. RNA-based HLA typing is low-coverage and unstable
- 899: 29 reads (exact reproduction); 902: 20 reads with a discordant call
  (A*02:01/B*58:01 vs A*02:06/B*53:01). Primary profile = 899 consensus.
- **Future:** clinical-grade DNA HLA typing (e.g. HLA-LA/OptiType on WES) before
  any patient-specific ranking is acted on.

## 5. Binding predictions are 9mer + MHCflurry-only
- 8/10/11mers not screened; NetMHCpan used only via archived IEDB tables
  (independent peptide set, BA IC50 2.5–12 nM), not a head-to-head benchmark;
  processing/presentation scores reported but no immunogenicity assay.
- **Future:** length-complete screen, NetMHCpan-4.1 + MHCflurry consensus,
  PRIME/TAP/immunogenicity modelling, then ELISpot/T-cell assays (required).

## 6. Expression gating is bulk RNA, TPM≥1, either replicate
- 1,047/1,083 retained. Bulk PDX signal dilutes subclonal/low-expressed neoantigens;
  no allele-specific expression or RNA VAF filter applied.
- **Future:** single-cell or allele-specific expression; RNA-VAF × DNA-VAF joint filter;
  proteasomal cleavage + TAP transport priors per candidate.

## 7. PDX + temozolomide context
- PDX stroma is murine (xenograft reads can misalign); temozolomide is mutagenic
  (therapy-induced variants ≠ truncal targets). Species-disambiguation (e.g. XenofilteR)
  and clonality analysis are future work.

## 8. Reproducibility boundaries
- VEP REST returns no allele frequencies in this Ensembl version (rsID proxy used);
  conda env pinned in 08_Reproducibility/gbm-pub.yml; runtime dirs (data/refs/results-RUNTIME)
  are git-ignored and regenerated by stage scripts; raw data retired with Last/
  (regenerate from SRA/ENSEMBL/UniProt public sources listed in the manifest).

## Recommended reviewer checklist
- [ ] Tumor–normal WES somatic confirmation of Table 1 v2 variants
- [ ] DNA-based HLA confirmation
- [ ] Sanger/IGV validation of lead variants
- [ ] ELISpot or multimer staining for top candidates
- [ ] Independent cohort replication
